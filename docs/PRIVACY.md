# Privacy

Nodvis Finance is self-hosted software. In a self-hosted deployment, the operator controls the database, backups, logs and network exposure. The core does not require external analytics, AI APIs or bank synchronization.

Financial records, account identifiers, household membership and authentication data are sensitive. Use HTTPS or a private network for remote access, protect PostgreSQL and backups, and do not share `.env` files. This document is product guidance, not a legal privacy notice or a claim of regulatory certification.
