# Nodvis Finance roadmap

This roadmap describes product direction without promising dates. Financial correctness, privacy and operability come before feature count.

## Now

- Make the first public self-hosted release reliable and understandable.
- Improve authenticated PL/EN UX across desktop and mobile.
- Expand migration, backup/restore and clean-install evidence.
- Collect real household feedback on transactions, obligations and forecast clarity.

## Next

- Forecast explanations for missing and stale account observations.
- Account balance history and clearer month-over-month cash-flow comparison.
- More polished import review and recovery workflows.
- Stronger session recovery and optional multi-factor authentication after a reviewed design.

## Later / ideas

- Additional deterministic import adapters.
- Budget-versus-actual reporting when category semantics are sufficiently mature.
- Safe hosted Finance convenience services.
- Further Nodvis products, including Recall, in their own repositories and release tracks.

Out of scope for this roadmap unless explicitly promoted: mandatory AI, unsafe public bank sync, generic document storage without a security design, and collapsing Nodvis Recall into Finance.
